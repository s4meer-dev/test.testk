Our Story

Born from a Love
of Great Coffee

Brew Haven started as a small corner cart in 2018, powered by a single dream — to serve coffee that feels like a warm hug. We source our beans directly from highland farms in Coorg and Chikmagalur, ensuring every cup carries the story of its origin.

Today, we're more than a café. We're a community — a place where ideas bloom over pour-overs, friendships deepen over cortados, and every visit becomes a cherished ritual.
